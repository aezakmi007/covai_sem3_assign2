# INSURANCE POLICY ADD-ONS, SCHEDULES, AND REGULATORY ANNEXURES

**Regulating Authority:** Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)  
**Applicability:** Extended Schedules for Optima Care Health, SecureDrive Motor, and Policyholder Protection Guidelines  

---

## SECTION 7: HEALTH POLICY DAY CARE PROCEDURES (ANNEXURE I)

### Clause 7.1: Microsurgical Eye Operations
Covers day-care cataract surgery, vitrectomy, trabeculectomy, retinal detachment surgery, and corneal surgery with laser treatment completed under 24 hours.

### Clause 7.2: ENT Surgical Procedures
Covers myringotomy, tympanoplasty, stapedectomy, mastoidectomy, turbinectomy, nasal polypectomy, and tonsillectomy performed under local or general anesthesia.

### Clause 7.3: Oncology Day Care Procedures
Covers outpatient and daycare intravenous chemotherapy, radiotherapy (including intensity-modulated radiation therapy and cyberknife), and brachytherapy.

### Clause 7.4: Urology and Nephrology Procedures
Covers lithotripsy (ESWL) for kidney stones, transurethral resection of the prostate (TURP), ureteroscopy (URS), and dialysis for acute renal failure.

### Clause 7.5: Orthopedic and Joint Day Care Interventions
Covers closed reduction of fractures, arthroscopic meniscus repair, carpal tunnel decompression, and removal of internal orthopedic fixation devices.

### Clause 7.6: Gastroenterology Day Care Interventions
Covers therapeutic endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP), endoscopic sclerotherapy for variceal bleeding, and polypectomy of colon.

## SECTION 8: HEALTH POLICY CRITICAL ILLNESS SPECIFICATIONS (ANNEXURE II)

### Clause 8.1: Cancer of Specified Severity
Covers malignant tumor characterized by the uncontrolled growth and spread of malignant cells with invasion and destruction of normal tissue, confirmed by histological biopsy.

### Clause 8.2: Myocardial Infarction (First Heart Attack)
Covers the death of a portion of the heart muscle arising from inadequate blood supply, evidenced by elevated cardiac enzymes (Troponin T/I) and new characteristic ECG changes.

### Clause 8.3: Open Heart Replacement or Repair of Heart Valves
Covers the actual undergoing of open-heart surgery to replace or repair one or more heart valves as certified by an independent cardiologist.

### Clause 8.4: Kidney Failure Requiring Regular Dialysis
Covers end-stage renal disease presenting as chronic irreversible failure of both kidneys to function, necessitating regular peritoneal or hemodialysis.

### Clause 8.5: Major Organ and Bone Marrow Transplant
Covers the actual undergoing of a transplant as a recipient for heart, lung, liver, kidney, pancreas, or human bone marrow using hematopoietic stem cells.

### Clause 8.6: Stroke Resulting in Permanent Symptoms
Covers any cerebrovascular incident producing neurological sequelae lasting at least 3 months, confirmed by modern neuro-imaging (MRI or CT scan).

## SECTION 9: MOTOR POLICY ADD-ON ENDORSEMENTS (SCHEDULE M)

### Clause 9.1: Zero Depreciation (Bumper to Bumper) Cover
In the event of an admissible claim under Section 1 (Own Damage), no depreciation deduction specified in Clause 1.2 will be applied to replaced parts including rubber, nylon, plastic, and fiberglass.

### Clause 9.2: Engine and Gearbox Protector Add-on
Covers repair or replacement of internal engine parts, gearbox components, differential, and power steering pump damaged solely by water ingression (hydrostatic lock) or lubricating oil leakage.

### Clause 9.3: Return to Invoice (RTI) Cover
In the event of Total Loss, Constructive Total Loss (Clause 1.4), or Theft (Clause 6.2), the Insurer will pay the difference between the Insured Declared Value (IDV) and the original on-road purchase price of the vehicle including registration and road tax.

### Clause 9.4: Consumables Expense Cover
Covers costs of consumables damaged or depleted in an accident, including engine oil, gearbox oil, brake fluid, coolant, AC refrigerant gas, nuts, bolts, washers, and oil filter.

### Clause 9.5: Roadside Assistance (RSA) Emergency Cover
Provides 24x7 roadside assistance services including flat tyre change, battery jumpstart, emergency fuel delivery (up to 5 litres), minor on-site mechanical repairs, and towing to nearest network garage up to 50 km.

### Clause 9.6: Key and Lock Replacement Cover
Reimburses the cost of replacing stolen or lost vehicle keys, including recoding of electronic vehicle immobilizer system and replacement of lock assembly up to ₹25,000 per policy year.

### Clause 9.7: Daily Commute / Loss of Use Allowance
Pays a daily fixed cash allowance of ₹1,000 per day (up to maximum 10 days) to cover local travel expenses while the insured vehicle is undergoing accidental repairs in an authorized garage.

### Clause 9.8: Tyre and Rim Protection Cover
Covers accidental damage to tyres (bulges, bursts, cut repairs) and alloy wheel rims where the damage is not accompanied by damage to the insured vehicle itself.

## SECTION 10: IRDAI OMBUDSMAN JURISDICTION & POWERS (ANNEXURE R)

### Clause 10.1: Territorial Ombudsman Centers
Establishes 17 Insurance Ombudsman offices across India (including Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, and Chandigarh) having exclusive territorial jurisdiction based on insurer branch location.

### Clause 10.2: Types of Complaints Entertained by Ombudsman
The Ombudsman entertains complaints relating to: delay in claim settlement, partial or total repudiation of claims, disputes over premium paid or payable, non-issuance of policy documents, and mis-selling.

### Clause 10.3: Ex-Gratia and Compensation Awards
The Ombudsman has statutory power to grant compensation for financial loss as well as harassment and mental agony, subject to the overall ceiling of ₹30,00,000 specified in Clause 3.3.

### Clause 10.4: Insurer Compliance Verification
If an insurer fails to comply with an Ombudsman award within 30 days of receiving the complainant's acceptance letter, the IRDAI may initiate supervisory enforcement action including regulatory penalties under Section 102 of the Insurance Act.
