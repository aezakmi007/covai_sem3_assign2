## SECTION 1: DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATIONS

### Clause 1.1: Accident
An accident means a sudden, unforeseen, and involuntary event caused by external, violent, and visible means.

### Clause 1.2: Admission
Admission means the formal entry and registration of the Insured Person into a registered Hospital for medically necessary treatment of an Illness or Injury.

### Clause 1.3: AYUSH Treatment
AYUSH Treatment refers to medical and/or hospitalization treatments given under Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy systems in a government hospital or recognized institute.

### Clause 1.4: Cashless Facility
Cashless Facility means a facility extended by the Insurer to the Insured where the medical expenses incurred by the Insured at a Network Hospital are directly settled by the Insurer to the Hospital, subject to policy terms and agreed tariff.

### Clause 1.5: Co-payment
Co-payment means a cost-sharing requirement under this health insurance policy that provides that the Policyholder/Insured will bear a specified percentage of the admissible claim amount. A Co-payment does not reduce the Sum Insured.

### Clause 1.6: Day Care Treatment
Day Care Treatment refers to medical treatment and/or surgical procedures undertaken under general or local anesthesia in a hospital/daycare center in less than 24 hours of admission because of technological advancements, which would otherwise have required hospitalization of more than 24 hours.

### Clause 1.7: Deductible
Deductible means a cost-sharing requirement that provides that the Insurer will not be liable for a specified rupee amount of the covered expenses, which will first be borne by the Insured Person before benefits become payable by the Insurer. A deductible does not reduce the Sum Insured.

### Clause 1.8: Domiciliary Hospitalization
Domiciliary Hospitalization means medical treatment for an illness/disease/injury which in the normal course would require care and treatment at a hospital but is actually taken while confined at home under specific medical conditions where the patient cannot be moved or hospital beds are unavailable.

### Clause 1.9: Grace Period
Grace Period means the specified period of 30 days immediately following the premium due date during which a payment can be made to renew or continue a policy in force without loss of continuity benefits such as waiting periods.

### Clause 1.10: Hospital
A Hospital means any institution established for in-patient care and day care treatment of illness and/or injuries and which has been registered as a hospital with local authorities under Clinical Establishments Act and complies with minimum bed criteria (at least 15 beds in towns with population > 10 lakhs, 10 beds in all other towns).

### Clause 1.11: Illness
Illness means a sickness or a disease or pathological condition leading to impairment of normal physiological function which manifests itself during the Policy Period and requires medical treatment.

### Clause 1.12: Injury
Injury means accidental physical bodily harm solely and directly caused by external, violent, visible, and evident means which is verified and certified by a registered Medical Practitioner.

### Clause 1.13: Inpatient Hospitalization
Inpatient Hospitalization means admission in a Hospital for a minimum period of 24 consecutive hours for Medically Necessary Treatment.

### Clause 1.14: Intensive Care Unit (ICU)
Intensive Care Unit means an identified section, ward, or wing of a hospital which is specially equipped with monitoring and life support systems and staffed with dedicated medical personnel for critically ill patients.

### Clause 1.15: Medically Necessary Treatment
Medically Necessary Treatment means any treatment, test, medication, or stay in hospital which is required for the medical management of illness or injury, conforms to standard medical practice, and is not solely for the convenience of the patient or doctor.

### Clause 1.16: Network Hospital
Network Hospital means hospitals, health care providers, or clinics enlisted by an Insurer or by a Third Party Administrator (TPA) to provide medical services to an Insured on a Cashless basis.

### Clause 1.17: Non-Network Hospital
Non-Network Hospital means any registered hospital or medical facility that is not part of the network of the Insurer or TPA.

### Clause 1.18: Pre-Existing Disease (PED)
Pre-Existing Disease means any condition, ailment, injury, or disease that is diagnosed by a physician within 36 months prior to the effective date of the policy issued by the Insurer or for which medical advice or treatment was recommended or received within 36 months prior to policy inception.

### Clause 1.19: Pre-Hospitalization Medical Expenses
Pre-Hospitalization Medical Expenses means medical expenses incurred immediately before the Insured Person is hospitalized, provided that such expenses are incurred for the same condition for which hospitalization occurred, up to a maximum period of 60 days.

### Clause 1.20: Post-Hospitalization Medical Expenses
Post-Hospitalization Medical Expenses means medical expenses incurred immediately after the Insured Person is discharged from the hospital, provided that such expenses are incurred for the same condition for which hospitalization occurred, up to a maximum period of 180 days.

### Clause 1.21: Reasonable and Customary Charges
Reasonable and Customary Charges means the charges for services or supplies which are standard and customary in the geographic area where the treatment is provided for similar illness or injury.

### Clause 1.22: Room Rent Limit
Room Rent Limit means the maximum daily amount covered by the Insurer towards room and boarding charges during inpatient hospitalization. Under Plan A, Room Rent is capped at 1% of Sum Insured per day. Under Plan B (Optima Super), Single Private Air Conditioned room is allowed with No Room Rent Capping.

### Clause 1.23: Sum Insured
Sum Insured means the pre-agreed maximum liability of the Insurer under this policy as specified in the Policy Schedule for any one policy year.

### Clause 1.24: Surgery or Surgical Procedure
Surgery means manual and/or operative procedure for correction of deformities, repair of injuries, or relief of suffering, performed by a qualified surgeon in an operating theater.

## SECTION 2: BASE COVERAGE BENEFITS

### Clause 2.1: Inpatient Hospitalization Expenses
The Insurer will cover medical expenses incurred for Inpatient Hospitalization exceeding 24 hours, including room boarding and nursing expenses, Intensive Care Unit (ICU) charges, fees of Medical Practitioner, Surgeon, Anesthetist, and Consultant, and charges for anesthesia, blood, oxygen, operation theater, surgical appliances, and diagnostic tests.

### Clause 2.2: Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Coverage
ICU charges are covered up to actual expenses under Plan B. Under Plan A, ICU coverage is capped at 2% of the Sum Insured per day. Pro-rata proportion deductions will apply to associate medical expenses if room rent exceeding eligibility is selected.

### Clause 2.3: Pre-Hospitalization Coverage
Pre-hospitalization medical expenses are reimbursed up to 60 days prior to the date of admission, provided the inpatient hospitalization claim is admissible under Clause 2.1.

### Clause 2.4: Post-Hospitalization Coverage
Post-hospitalization medical expenses are reimbursed up to 180 days following the date of discharge from the hospital, provided the primary hospitalization claim is admissible.

### Clause 2.5: Day Care Procedures
Medical expenses incurred for all Day Care Procedures specified in Annexure I (over 500 procedures) are covered up to the Sum Insured, even though hospitalization is less than 24 hours, provided modern technological advancement necessitated shorter stay. Outpatient consultations are not covered under this clause.

### Clause 2.6: Domiciliary Hospitalization Benefit
Domiciliary hospitalization is covered up to a maximum of 10% of the Sum Insured subject to a maximum of ₹50,000 per policy year, provided medical treatment continues for at least 3 consecutive days and the condition fulfills the definition under Clause 1.8. Bronchitis, Asthma, Cough and Cold, and Diarrhea are excluded from Domiciliary benefits.

### Clause 2.7: Road Ambulance Cover
The policy covers road ambulance expenses incurred for transfer to the nearest hospital with adequate medical facilities, subject to a limit of ₹2,500 per hospitalization and a maximum of ₹5,000 per policy year.

### Clause 2.8: AYUSH Inpatient Treatment
Inpatient treatment taken under recognized AYUSH systems in a government AYUSH hospital or accredited institute is covered up to 100% of the Sum Insured without sub-limits, provided treatment is for an illness that otherwise qualifies for inpatient hospitalization.

### Clause 2.9: Organ Donor Expenses
Medical expenses incurred for in-patient hospitalization of an organ donor harvesting organ for the Insured Person are covered up to the Sum Insured, provided the organ is donated in compliance with Transplantation of Human Organs Act, 1994. Screening expenses of donors are excluded.

### Clause 2.10: Cumulative Bonus (No Claim Bonus)
For every claim-free policy year, the Sum Insured shall be increased by 10% up to a maximum of 100% of the basic Sum Insured, provided the policy is renewed without any break. In the event of a claim, the accumulated bonus is reduced by 10% at renewal, but the base Sum Insured remains untouched.

### Clause 2.11: Restoration of Sum Insured
If the base Sum Insured and Cumulative Bonus are exhausted during the policy year due to claims paid, a 100% restoration of the base Sum Insured will be triggered automatically once per policy year for unrelated illnesses or injuries. Restoration cannot be utilized for the same illness for which a claim was paid within the same year.

## SECTION 3: WAITING PERIODS

### Clause 3.1: Initial Waiting Period (30 Days)
A waiting period of 30 days from the inception date of the policy applies to all claims, except accidental bodily injury. Any illness diagnosed or manifesting during the first 30 days of coverage is permanently excluded from coverage under this policy.

### Clause 3.2: Specific Disease Waiting Period (24 Months)
A mandatory waiting period of 24 consecutive months of continuous coverage applies to the following listed conditions: Benign prostatic hypertrophy, Hernia, Hydrocele, Fistula and Fissure in ano, Piles, Sinusitis, Tonsillitis, Gall bladder stones, Renal calculi, Cataract, Hysterectomy for non-malignant conditions, Joint replacement surgeries unless due to accident, Osteoarthritis, and Spinal disc disorders.

### Clause 3.3: Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period (36 Months)
Medical expenses directly or indirectly related to any Pre-Existing Disease (PED) as defined under Clause 1.18 will not be covered until 36 months of continuous coverage have elapsed since the inception of the first policy with the Insurer. If porting from another insurer, continuity benefit on the elapsed waiting period is credited as per IRDAI portability guidelines.

### Clause 3.4: Moratorium Period (60 Months)
After 60 continuous months of coverage under this policy, no claim shall be contestable by the Insurer on grounds of misrepresentation or non-disclosure of health conditions, except in instances of proven fraud.

## SECTION 4: DEDUCTIBLES, CO-PAYMENTS, AND LIMITS

### Clause 4.1: Standard Deductible Option
Where the policyholder opts for a Voluntary Deductible of ₹10,000 or ₹25,000 to reduce annual premium, the Insured must bear the selected deductible amount on aggregate admissible claims during each policy year before the Insurer pays any claim benefit.

### Clause 4.2: Senior Citizen Co-payment
A compulsory co-payment of 10% applies to all admissible claims if the Insured Person enters the policy at or after age 61. The 10% co-payment is deducted from the net admissible claim after applying deductibles.

### Clause 4.3: Cataract Sub-limit
Coverage for Cataract surgery is limited to a maximum of ₹40,000 per eye under Plan A, and ₹60,000 per eye under Plan B, inclusive of lens costs and surgeon fees.

### Clause 4.4: Modern Treatment Methods Sub-limits
Modern treatments such as Robotic Surgeries, Oral Chemotherapy, Immunotherapy, and Stem Cell Therapy are covered up to 50% of the Sum Insured or ₹5,00,000, whichever is lower.

## SECTION 5: GENERAL EXCLUSIONS

### Clause 5.1: Investigation and Evaluation Only
Expenses related to admission primarily for diagnostic, X-ray, or laboratory examinations with no active medical or surgical treatment are excluded.

### Clause 5.2: Rest Cure, Rehabilitation, and Respite Care
Expenses related to any admission primarily for bed rest, convalescence, rehabilitation, or nursing care at home or health resort are excluded.

### Clause 5.3: Obesity and Weight Control
Medical expenses for surgical or medical treatment of obesity or weight reduction are excluded unless BMI is greater than 40 and accompanied by life-threatening comorbidities as certified by a specialist.

### Clause 5.4: Cosmetic or Plastic Surgery
Expenses for cosmetic or aesthetic treatments, facelifts, or plastic surgeries are excluded unless necessitated by accidental injury, burns, or cancer reconstruction.

### Clause 5.5: Hazardous and Adventure Sports
Treatment expenses arising from participation as a professional in hazardous or adventure sports such as skydiving, motor racing, scuba diving, and mountaineering are excluded.

### Clause 5.6: Breach of Law
Expenses incurred for treatment directly caused by or arising from the Insured Person committing or attempting to commit an intentional breach of law with criminal intent.

### Clause 5.7: Substance Abuse and Intoxication
Expenses incurred for treatment directly or indirectly attributable to alcohol abuse, drug abuse, or use of intoxicating substances without prescription are excluded.

### Clause 5.8: Unproven Treatments and Experimental Therapies
Expenses for treatments, procedures, or medicines that are not recognized by standard medical boards or that are undergoing clinical trials are excluded.

### Clause 5.9: Maternity and Pregnancy Expenses
Medical expenses related to pregnancy, childbirth, caesarean section, abortion, or miscarriage are excluded, except ectopic pregnancy diagnosed by clinical ultrasound.

### Clause 5.10: Dental Treatment
Dental treatment or surgery of any kind is excluded unless requiring hospitalization following an accidental bodily injury.

## SECTION 6: CLAIMS PROCEDURE AND DOCUMENTATION

### Clause 6.1: Intimation of Cashless Claim
For planned hospitalization at a Network Hospital, pre-authorization must be submitted at least 48 hours prior to admission. In emergency hospitalization, claim intimation must be given within 24 hours of hospital admission.

### Clause 6.2: Intimation of Reimbursement Claim
For treatment at a Non-Network Hospital, written intimation of claim must be submitted to the Insurer or TPA within 48 hours of admission or prior to discharge, whichever is earlier.

### Clause 6.3: Claim Submission Deadline
All original bills, receipts, discharge summary, diagnostic reports, and medical prescriptions must be submitted to the Insurer within 30 days from the date of discharge from the hospital.

### Clause 6.4: Post-Hospitalization Claim Submission
Bills and receipts for post-hospitalization medical expenses covered under Clause 2.4 must be submitted within 30 days of completion of the 180-day post-hospitalization period.

### Clause 6.5: Required Claim Documents
A valid reimbursement claim dossier must include: Completed signed Claim Form Part A and B, Original Discharge Summary, Original Final Hospital Bill with itemized break-up, Original Payment Receipts, Pharmacy bills with supporting prescriptions, Doctor consultation sheets, and KYC documents for claims above ₹1,00,000.

### Clause 6.6: Claim Settlement Timelines
The Insurer shall settle or reject a claim within 30 days from the date of receipt of the last necessary document. In case of delay, the Insurer is liable to pay interest at 2% above the bank rate as mandated by IRDAI.
