# IRDAI MASTER CIRCULAR ON HEALTH AND GENERAL INSURANCE OPERATIONS (2024)

**Issuing Authority:** Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)  
**Circular Reference:** IRDAI/HLT/CIR/2024/08  
**Applicability:** All General and Standalone Health Insurers  

---

## SECTION 14: CASHLESS EVERYWHERE INITIATIVE & DISCHARGE TIMELINES

### Clause 14.1: Cashless Everywhere Nationwide Mandate
Every health insurer must provide cashless hospitalization facility to policyholders even at non-network hospitals, provided the hospital has at least 15 functional in-patient beds and agrees to the insurer's standard medical pricing tariff.

### Clause 14.2: One-Hour Pre-Authorization Timeline
The insurer or Third Party Administrator (TPA) must communicate its approval or query for cashless pre-authorization within 1 hour of receiving the request from the hospital.

### Clause 14.3: Three-Hour Final Discharge Clearance Mandate
Upon receiving the final hospital bill and discharge summary, the insurer or TPA must grant final authorization and clear the claim within 3 hours. Any hospital retention charges caused by insurer delay exceeding 3 hours must be borne solely by the insurer.

### Clause 14.4: Senior Citizen Priority Claims Desk
All insurers must maintain a dedicated senior citizen customer care desk and ensure that claims lodged by insured persons aged 60 and above are processed by priority adjusters within 15 days.

## SECTION 15: MOTOR CLAIMS SETTLEMENT AND SALVAGE RULES

### Clause 15.1: Cashless Garage Network Everywhere
For motor accidental claims, insurers must establish tie-ups with authorized and multi-brand workshops ensuring direct cashless settlement of admissible repair costs less deductibles.

### Clause 15.2: 48-Hour Surveyor Inspection Mandate
Upon receiving accidental motor claim intimation, an independent surveyor must physically inspect the vehicle within 48 hours in metro cities and 72 hours in non-metro areas.

### Clause 15.3: Fast-Track Assessment for Minor Losses
Accidental Own Damage claims with estimated damage below ₹20,000 may be processed on self-declaration and video survey without deputing a licensed physical surveyor.

### Clause 15.4: Salvage Value Realization on Total Loss
In total loss and constructive total loss (CTL) settlements under Clause 1.4, the salvage of the damaged vehicle can be retained by the insured with a mutually agreed deduction from the IDV payout, or surrendered to the insurer.

## SECTION 16: POLICYHOLDER PROTECTION AND DIGITAL REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

### Clause 16.1: Direct Bank Transfer for Free-Look Refunds
Refunds resulting from free-look cancellations under Clause 1.1 must be credited electronically via NEFT/RTGS to the policyholder's registered bank account within 7 business days.

### Clause 16.2: Prohibition of Mid-Term Cancellation by Insurer
An insurer cannot cancel an active health insurance policy during the policy year on grounds of multiple claims or adverse claims experience.

### Clause 16.3: Digital ABHA and e-Insurance Account Integration
All policyholders are entitled to store and view their health and general insurance contracts in electronic form through authorized Insurance Repositories linked to their Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA).

### Clause 16.4: Zero Deductions on Electronic Hospitalization Records
Where cashless claims are verified electronically through the National Health Claims Exchange (NHCX), no administrative handling fees or documentation charges may be levied on the insured.
