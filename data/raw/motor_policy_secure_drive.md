## SECTION 1: LOSS OR DAMAGE TO THE VEHICLE INSURED (OWN DAMAGE)

### Clause 1.1: Scope of Own Damage Cover
The Insurer will indemnify the Insured against loss or damage to the private vehicle insured and its accessories whilst thereon by:
1. Fire, explosion, self-ignition, or lightning.
2. Burglary, housebreaking, or theft.
3. Riot and strike.
4. Earthquake (fire and shock damage).
5. Flood, typhoon, hurricane, storm, tempest, inundation, cyclone, hailstorm, and frost.
6. Accidental external means.
7. Malicious act.
8. Terrorist activity.
9. Whilst in transit by road, rail, inland waterway, lift, elevator, or air.
10. Landslide and rockslide.

### Clause 1.2: Depreciation Scale on Parts for Partial Loss Claims
In the settlement of claims for accidental partial loss, depreciation shall be deducted on parts replaced at the following rates:
1. Rubber, nylon, plastic parts, tyres, tubes, batteries, and airbags: 50% depreciation.
2. Fiberglass components: 30% depreciation.
3. All parts made of glass: Nil (0% depreciation).
4. All other parts made of steel/metal:
   - Vehicle age up to 6 months: Nil depreciation.
   - Vehicle age > 6 months up to 1 year: 5% depreciation.
   - Vehicle age > 1 year up to 2 years: 10% depreciation.
   - Vehicle age > 2 years up to 3 years: 15% depreciation.
   - Vehicle age > 3 years up to 4 years: 25% depreciation.
   - Vehicle age > 4 years up to 5 years: 35% depreciation.
   - Vehicle age > 5 years up to 10 years: 40% depreciation.
   - Vehicle age over 10 years: 50% depreciation.

### Clause 1.3: Insured Declared Value (IDV) Definition
The Insured Declared Value (IDV) of the vehicle shall be treated as the 'Sum Insured' for the purpose of this policy. The IDV is fixed at the commencement of each policy period for the insured vehicle on the basis of the manufacturer's listed selling price less depreciation based on vehicle age.

### Clause 1.4: Total Loss and Constructive Total Loss (CTL)
A vehicle will be considered to be a Constructive Total Loss (CTL) where the aggregate cost of retrieval and/or repair of the vehicle, subject to terms and conditions of the policy, exceeds 75% of the Insured Declared Value (IDV) of the vehicle. In the event of Total Loss or CTL, the Insurer pays the full IDV after surrendering vehicle salvage and registration papers.

### Clause 1.5: Towing and Protection Expenses
If the insured vehicle is disabled by reason of accidental damage, the Insurer will bear the reasonable cost of protection and removal to the nearest repairer up to a maximum limit of ₹1,500 for private cars. Any additional towing charges must be borne by the Insured unless the Roadside Assistance add-on was purchased.

## SECTION 2: LIABILITY TO THIRD PARTIES

### Clause 2.1: Third Party Bodily Injury or Death Cover
Subject to the limits of liability, the Insurer will indemnify the Insured in the event of an accident caused by or arising out of the use of the vehicle against all sums including claimant's costs and expenses which the Insured shall become legally liable to pay in respect of death of or bodily injury to any third party person. In accordance with Motor Vehicles Act, liability for bodily injury or death is unlimited.

### Clause 2.2: Third Party Property Damage (TPPD) Limit
The Insurer indemnifies damage to property belonging to any third party person up to a maximum statutory limit of ₹7,50,000 in respect of any one accident or series of accidents arising out of one event. The Policyholder may opt for a restricted TPPD limit of ₹6,000 to obtain a minor premium discount.

### Clause 2.3: Legal Expenses in Third Party Claims
The Insurer will pay all reasonable legal costs and expenses incurred with its prior written consent in defending any suit or criminal prosecution brought against the Insured or the Authorized Driver in connection with an accident.

## SECTION 3: COMPULSORY PERSONAL ACCIDENT COVER FOR OWNER-DRIVER

### Clause 3.1: Compulsory Personal Accident (CPA) Scope
The Insurer undertakes to pay compensation up to a Capital Sum Insured of ₹15,00,000 for bodily injury or death sustained by the Owner-Driver of the vehicle in direct connection with the insured vehicle whilst driving, mounting into, or dismounting from the vehicle.

### Clause 3.2: CPA Scale of Compensation
Compensation under CPA is payable as follows:
1. Death: 100% of Capital Sum Insured (₹15,00,000).
2. Loss of two limbs or sight of two eyes: 100% of Capital Sum Insured (₹15,00,000).
3. Loss of one limb or sight of one eye: 50% of Capital Sum Insured (₹7,50,000).
4. Permanent total disablement from injuries: 100% of Capital Sum Insured (₹15,00,000).

### Clause 3.3: Exemption from CPA Cover
An owner-driver may opt out of the Compulsory Personal Accident cover if they already possess an effective standalone 24-hour Personal Accident cover with Sum Insured of at least ₹15,00,000, or own another vehicle with valid CPA cover.

## SECTION 4: DEDUCTIBLES AND DISCOUNTS

### Clause 4.1: Compulsory Deductible
The Insurer shall not be liable for each and every claim arising under Section 1 (Own Damage) in respect of the compulsory deductible:
- For private cars with engine capacity not exceeding 1,500 cc: ₹1,000 per claim.
- For private cars with engine capacity exceeding 1,500 cc: ₹2,000 per claim.

### Clause 4.2: Voluntary Deductible
If the Insured opts for an additional Voluntary Deductible at policy issuance, the Insured shall bear that amount on each claim in addition to the Compulsory Deductible, in exchange for a premium discount on the basic Own Damage premium:
- Voluntary Deductible ₹2,500: 20% discount on OD premium (max ₹750).
- Voluntary Deductible ₹5,000: 25% discount on OD premium (max ₹1,500).
- Voluntary Deductible ₹7,500: 30% discount on OD premium (max ₹2,000).
- Voluntary Deductible ₹15,000: 35% discount on OD premium (max ₹2,500).

### Clause 4.3: No Claim Bonus (NCB) Scale
No Claim Bonus (NCB) is earned on the Own Damage premium for every claim-free year according to the following progressive scale:
- 1 claim-free year: 20% discount.
- 2 consecutive claim-free years: 25% discount.
- 3 consecutive claim-free years: 35% discount.
- 4 consecutive claim-free years: 45% discount.
- 5 consecutive claim-free years: 50% maximum discount.
If a claim is lodged during the policy period, the NCB is reduced to Nil (0%) at the subsequent renewal, unless a Zero NCB Protection add-on was in effect.

### Clause 4.4: Transfer of No Claim Bonus
NCB belongs to the Insured (vehicle owner) and not to the vehicle. Upon sale of the insured vehicle, the Insured can retain the NCB certificate and apply the earned discount to a replacement vehicle purchased within 3 years.

## SECTION 5: GENERAL EXCLUSIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL SECTIONS

### Clause 5.1: Contractual Liability Exclusion
The policy does not cover any liability arising out of an agreement or contract entered into by the Insured which would not have attached in the absence of such contract.

### Clause 5.2: Consequential Loss and Depreciation
The Insurer is not liable for any consequential financial loss, depreciation, wear and tear, or mechanical or electrical breakdown, failure, or breakage.

### Clause 5.3: Driving Under the Influence (DUI)
The Insurer shall not be liable for any accidental loss, damage, or third-party liability caused whilst the vehicle is being driven by any person under the influence of intoxicating liquor or drugs. A blood alcohol content exceeding 30 mg per 100 ml of blood constitutes statutory intoxication.

### Clause 5.4: Driving Without Valid Driving License
The Insurer is not liable if the vehicle is being driven by any person who is not holding an effective and valid driving license at the time of the accident, or by a person disqualified from holding or obtaining such a license.

### Clause 5.5: Geographic and War Limitations
Any accidental loss or damage occurring outside the geographical boundaries of India is excluded, unless specifically extended by endorsement to Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, or Sri Lanka. Loss due to war, invasion, or nuclear weapons is excluded.

### Clause 5.6: Speed Contests and Motor Rallies
Any loss, damage, or liability incurred whilst the insured vehicle is used for racing, pacemaking, speed testing, reliability trials, or motor rallies is excluded.

### Clause 5.7: Hydrostatic Lock and Engine Inundation
Damage caused to the engine of the vehicle due to water ingression or hydrostatic lock caused by attempting to restart an inundated vehicle is excluded under base cover, unless an Engine Protector add-on is active.

## SECTION 6: CLAIMS SETTLEMENT AND INTIMATION RULES

### Clause 6.1: Notice of Accidental Loss
Notice shall be given in writing to the Insurer immediately upon the occurrence of any accidental loss or damage, and in the event of any claim, within 48 to 72 hours of the incident.

### Clause 6.2: Theft and Burglary Procedure
In the event of theft or criminal act, immediate notice must be given to the local Police authorities and a First Information Report (FIR) must be registered. A final untraced report (Non-Traceable Certificate) certified by the judicial magistrate must be submitted to the Insurer before a total theft claim can be settled.

### Clause 6.3: Independent Surveyor Appointment
For any Own Damage claim where the estimated loss exceeds ₹50,000, an IRDAI-licensed independent insurance surveyor must be deputed to inspect the vehicle before dismantling and repairs commence.

### Clause 6.4: Cashless Workshop Settlement
Under the Cashless garage network, the Insurer directly settles approved repair costs with the authorized workshop, less the applicable depreciation (Clause 1.2), compulsory deductible (Clause 4.1), and salvage value.
